      Method

      How this review ran

      Eleven reviewers ran in parallel, one per contract family plus one for upgrade and rollout safety. Each read the 2026-10-02 audit, its fix plan and the principal-resolution spec first. Each then traced every state-changing method, inner transaction and caller-provided transaction in its slice.

      The review was read-only. Nobody built, edited, committed or sent anything. No LocalNet or end-to-end tests ran, because other sessions share LocalNet. Pure unit tests ran where they exist: PluginDex bins and size (12/12), Auction/Raffle/Marketplace static tests (25/25), Social legacy ABI (14/14), Guild consensus ABI (5/5). MainNet was queried read-only through public algod and indexer endpoints.

      The coordinator then re-read the code for every Critical and High finding and most Medium findings. The “Check” column records the result. “Not re-checked” means only the reviewer traced it.

      Reviewed tree: HEAD e7f174ee plus the uncommitted working tree as of 2026-10-05. Full per-slice reports live in projects/akita-sc/audits/review-2026-10-05/.
